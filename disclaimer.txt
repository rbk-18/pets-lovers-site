1. PRODUCT SAFETY AND SUPERVISION
- Always supervise your pet while it is on or near the grooming hammock. Never leave a pet unattended on the product, and never leave the straps fastened when you step away.
- Use the product only with cats and small dogs that fit comfortably within the product's dimensions. Check the listed dimensions before use. Do not overload the product.
- Assemble the product fully and correctly according to the instructions before each use. Check that all poles and connectors are firmly seated.
- Place the product on a flat, stable, non-slip surface. Do not use it on elevated surfaces from which it could fall unless the product is fully stable.
- Fasten straps snugly but not tightly. Make sure your pet can breathe freely and that straps do not press on the neck, throat or joints.
- Stop using the product immediately if your pet shows signs of distress, panic, pain, difficulty breathing or injury.
- Inspect the product before each use. Do not use it if any part is damaged, loose or worn.
- Keep small parts and packaging away from children and pets.
