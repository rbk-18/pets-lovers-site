Questions, answered

Will my pet actually stay calm on it?
Most pets settle once the padded straps hold them snug and they realize they can't push off. Let them sniff it first, keep the first session short with a treat, and build up.
Is it comfortable?
Your pet lies on soft, breathable mesh with the weight spread evenly. Legs hang naturally through the openings and the straps are padded. Always stay with your pet while they're on it.
What size is it? Will it fit my pet?
The bed is 23.6 × 13.0 in and stands 15.7 in tall (60 × 33 × 40 cm). It's made for cats and small dogs. If your pet lies comfortably within 23 in, it fits. Unsure? Message us the breed and weight.
Is it sturdy? Will it tip over?
The frame is stainless steel with a wide base. Use it on a flat surface: the floor, a table or a countertop.
Does it work for cats?
Yes. Cats do especially well because the straps keep them steady and their claws away from you.
What colors are available?
Black, Pink, Blue and Khaki. The pink is a soft, calm shade rather than a bright one.
Is it easy to set up?
No tools needed. The poles slot into the connectors in a few minutes (see the video), and it comes apart again for compact storage.
How do I clean it?
Wipe or rinse the mesh and let it air-dry. It dries quickly.
