This Refund and Return Policy is part of, and is incorporated into, our Terms of Service. Please read it carefully before placing an order. By placing an order you confirm that you have read and agree to this policy.

1. SUMMARY
- All sales are final except as described in this policy.
- We do not accept returns for change of mind, wrong size selection, color preference or because your pet did not like the product.
- We handle claims for items that arrive damaged, defective, incomplete or different from what you ordered, if reported within 7 days of delivery and supported by the evidence listed below.
- An unboxing video is required for claims of damage, missing parts or wrong item.

2. ITEMS NOT ELIGIBLE FOR RETURN OR REFUND
The following are not eligible for a return, refund or replacement:
a) Change of mind, or ordering the wrong color, quantity or bundle.
b) A pet's reaction, behavior or unwillingness to use the product. Every animal is different, and we do not guarantee any particular result with your pet.
c) Products that do not fit your pet because the customer did not check the listed dimensions before purchase. Product dimensions are listed on the product page.
d) Minor differences between the product received and product photos, videos or social media content, as described in Section 6.
e) Products that have been used, washed, assembled and disassembled repeatedly, altered, damaged by pets (including chewing, scratching and soiling), damaged through misuse, improper assembly, overloading, or use contrary to the product instructions or its intended purpose.
f) Products with normal wear and tear.
g) Products opened without a continuous unboxing video, where the claim concerns damage, missing parts or a wrong item.
h) Items purchased on final sale or with a discount code marked as final sale.
i) Claims submitted more than 7 days after the carrier's delivery date.
j) Packages delayed, lost or stolen as described in our Shipping Policy, except where that policy provides otherwise.

3. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, INCOMPLETE OR WRONG ITEMS
3.1. If your item arrives damaged, has a manufacturing defect, is missing parts or is not the item you ordered, contact us at support@yourstore.com within 7 days of delivery.
3.2. Your claim must include:
- Your order number and the name on the order;
- A description of the problem;
- Clear photos of the item, the problem, the shipping label and the outer packaging;
- For damage, missing parts or wrong item: a continuous, unedited video that starts with the sealed package with the shipping label visible and shows the complete opening of the package and its contents. Videos that are cut, edited or start after the package has been opened may not be accepted.
- For manufacturing defects that appear during first use: a video that clearly shows the defect.
3.3. We will review your claim, usually within 3 business days, and may request additional information. We reserve the right to decide whether a claim is approved based on the evidence provided.
3.4. If the claim is approved, we will, at our sole discretion, send a replacement item or part, issue a partial refund, or issue a full refund. In most cases we do not require you to send the item back. If we do request a return, we will provide instructions and the return address, and the item must be returned complete, in its original packaging, within 14 days of our instructions.
3.5. Items sent back to us without prior approval will not be accepted or refunded.

4. QUALITY GUARANTEE
4.1. Our "Quality Guarantee" means that we stand behind our products against manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship present at the time of delivery, reported within 7 days of delivery and handled under Section 3 of this policy.
4.2. The Quality Guarantee does not cover the situations listed in Section 2, does not extend the claim period, and is not a money-back or satisfaction guarantee.

5. REFUNDS
5.1. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once issued, refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.
5.2. Original shipping charges (if any) and shipping protection fees are non-refundable, except where a refund is issued because we sent a damaged, defective or wrong item.
5.3. If a return is requested and approved for reasons other than our error, return shipping costs are paid by the customer, and we may deduct any costs we incurred to ship the item to you.
5.4. Partial refunds may be issued for items that are not in their original condition, are damaged or have missing parts for reasons not due to our error.
